# M1: Design system, logo concepts, base layout, home page

## Goal

Design system (tokens, fonts, base components), three logo concepts, the base
layout (header, navigation, footer) and the home page with illustrated
placeholders.

Done when: the home page renders on a phone and on desktop with the design
system, the three logo concepts and screenshots are shown, and Kristjan
approves (CHECKPOINT).

Status: approved at the checkpoint (2026-11-23): logo concept A (krava),
design direction and copy facts confirmed. The kangla and kozolec emblems
were removed.

## Design plan

- **Subject and audience**: a small family dairy farm selling to people who
  order from their phones, once a week. The page's main job is "see this week's
  offer and order"; its second job is trust: handmade and honest.
- **Palette** (from the brief, contrast checked): cream paper `#F7F1E5`, meadow
  green `#3E5C3A` as the brand colour, ink `#2E241B`, rust `#C56B2C` for stamps
  and hand-drawn marks only, butter `#E9C46A` for highlights. Added: a darker
  rust `#9C4F1C` for small rust text (5.3:1), `#8A7A66` for form borders
  (3.7:1), `#EFE4CD` for a deeper cream band, two meadow tints for
  illustrations only.
- **Type**: Fraunces with its SOFT axis at 100 for headings (rounded, buttery
  serifs that suit a dairy), Source Sans 3 for body text. Both self-hosted,
  latin and latin-ext (č, š, ž). Fluid scale with `clamp()`.
- **Layout**: mobile first, single column, left aligned. On desktop the hero
  and the offer use two columns. Sections are separated by a hand-drawn line of
  rolling hills instead of straight rules.
- **The one bold element**: the round rust "Domače" stamp pressed onto the
  corner of the hero picture, with a single stamp-press animation on load
  (off under `prefers-reduced-motion`). Everything else stays quiet.
- **Avoided on purpose**: identical product cards (the offer is a price list
  with dotted leaders), uppercase labels above headings, arrows in buttons.
  The steps are numbered because ordering really is a sequence.

## Decisions

- **`npm ci` fix (affects M0)**: the M0 lockfile made `npm ci` fail
  (`@types/node` 18, needed by `cloudflare` inside Wrangler, clashed with the
  optional peers of Vite and Vitest). `package.json` now has
  `"overrides": { "@types/node": "22.20.5" }`. No package was added; it pins a
  transitive type package. The lockfile is generated with
  `npm install --package-lock-only` in a clean folder, so it keeps the macOS
  binaries. `npm ci` checked on Linux x64 and arm64.
- Logo: concept A (krava), chosen at the checkpoint. It is used for the
  horizontal logo (`Logo.astro`), the round stamp (`LogoStamp.astro`) and the
  favicon.
- Illustrations are inline SVG components. Their colours come from tokens
  through role classes (`ill-hill-near`, `ill-sun` and so on), because CSS
  variables do not work in SVG attributes and inline styles are not allowed.
- `public/favicon.svg` has its two colours written as hex values: a favicon is
  a standalone file and cannot read the tokens.
- Component `Props` interfaces stay inside their `.astro` component (Astro's
  convention, like an Angular `@Input`). Data shapes shared across files live
  in `src/models`.
- The home offer shows four sample lines (product names from the brief,
  TODO units and prices, sample stock) for the design review. M4 replaces them
  with the open round. The news section shows its empty state until news exist
  (M5).
- The stamp lettering uses tokens in SVG units (`--stamp-text-size`); the
  stamp counts as a logo, so its rust lettering has no contrast requirement.
